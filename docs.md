Who we are
Christverse is a Christian community focused on the spread of the gospel of Christ through community gatherings and initiatives that are also driven at raising young, impactful kingdom influencers in their sphere of influence.

Mission Statement
- To empower believers to speak life and faith into their daily lives through consistent and intentional confession of God's Word.
- To create a community that fosters spiritual growth, encouragement, and accountability.
- To help believers develop a habit of declaring God's truth over their lives, leading to a deeper faith, hope, and transformation.

Vision Statement
- To see a global community of believers who confidently declare God's Word, living victorious, successful and joyful lives, and transforming their world for Christ.
- To raise an army of young, rich, righteous, counter-culture and narrative changing believers and influencers for Christ using every avenue available.
- To create a movement of faith-filled confessions that inspires believers to live boldly, love unconditionally, and serve selflessly.

Our purpose
- The purpose of Christverse is to know Christ and to make Him known through the power of faith declarations and healthy Christian community

Reach
- We intend to have a reach and membership of 1 billion people in ten thousand cities. The scope of our mission is global in scope and therefore all our plans must reflect it.

Departments and initiatives.
There are currently 8 departments and initiatives in Christverse globally
1. iConnect: This is a platform that is specifically created for social media influencers, content creators, brand identity designers who are partnered or under the oversight of Christverse globally.
2. iCurate: This is specifically for writers, authors, spoken word artists, script writers and affirmation enthusiasts who would like to be part of the team curating the daily confessions as well and the upcoming devotional.
3. iCreate: This is specifically a collection of actors, actresses, singers, songwriters, tech savvy individuals, artists, dancers, painters, cooks, bakers, photographers and graphic designers.
4. Camp Elihu: This is the official mentorship platform of the Christverse. Targeted at youth and personal development through sound teaching and mentorship programs. The aim is to raise young, rich, righteous, rulers for Christ in every sphere of influence.
5. TBT podcast: This is the official podcast program and affiliate of Christverse that is centered on creating rich conversations on faith, culture and Jesus. It is an avenue to expose young believers to edifying Christian content.
6. Colors: This is the official hangout and outreach program for Christverse globally. This hangout can take several forms depending on what is suitable at the moment.
7. Harmony: This is our daily devotional that teaches the gospel of Christ deepening the believers walk with the Father as well as providing inspiration for the daily faith life and mission minded Christianity.
8. Freeform: This is our monthly virtual or physical meeting with community members and it can be held by teams in different locations. It fosters team bonding and creates a real sense of comradery among the community members.
